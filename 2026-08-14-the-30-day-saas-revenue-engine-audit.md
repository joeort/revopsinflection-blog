### 1\. **AUDIT** | _Exposing Tech Debt and Systems Bloat_

Before you can build high-velocity pipeline, you have to inspect the structural integrity of your core engine. In most mid-market SaaS companies, Salesforce (SFDC) has become an archive of legacy decisions. It's bloated with custom objects, broken validation rules, and orphaned tools dragging down GTM efficiency.

  * **Systems Bloat:** We inspect tech stack utilization. Are you paying $200k/year for point solutions that native SFDC functionality or core MAP features could handle?

  * **Data Completeness:** We audit required field compliance across core pipeline objects. If account-level hierarchy and lead-to-account mapping are broken, full-funnel attribution is impossible. What you track is just as important as what you aren't tracking (I'm looking at you, Reason codes).

  * **The Valuation Impact:** Eliminating tech stack waste immediately improves EBITDA while laying the foundation for trustworthy reporting.




### 2\. **ANALYZE** | _Surgical Funnel & Pricing Diagnostics_

Operating partners look at numbers differently than departmental leaders. We don't care about top-of-funnel vanity metrics; we care about conversion decay, unit economics, and yield.

  * **The Pricing Expansion Gap:** One of the fastest value creation levers in portfolio management is identifying long-tenured customers paying steep discounts relative to your current list price. 

  * **Funnel Velocity & Decay:** A deep dive into your funnel conversions and velocities to see where things are getting stuck. Improving conversions in your lowest performing step can have huge impacts down the funnel.

  * **The Quota and Bookings Model:** We review any existing planning docs for the assumptions used and any gaps (it's typically rep attrition or unrealistic ramp schedules). Knowing early on that you'll be short on quota capacity will make the conversation easier with the Board than waiting until Q4.




### 3\. **ALIGN** | _Setting Priorities_

If the Analyze step can identify the highest potential impacts rather than the most immediate pain of a company, how do you determine which project to tackle first? That's where the Align phase shines. It brings together the key functional leaders to review the findings and build a gameplan to execute against. It typically has items like:

  * **Unifying GTM Taxonomy:** We build a single, cross-functional lifecycle definition across Sales, Marketing, and CS.

  * **Handoff SLAs:** We look to build the systems so we can monitor and measure the ability to achieve SLAs between teams. Setting SLAs but not reporting on them is the same as not having them at all.




### 4\. **ARCHITECT** | _Building Scalable Infrastructure & Models_

Once the gaps are exposed and the teams are aligned on priorities, we design the core mechanics required to scale predictably. This is where mathematical rigor replaces executive guesswork.

  * **Scientific Capacity Planning:** We move away from simple headcount math. We build capacity models factoring in rep ramp schedules, rep attrition, rep productivity, and marketing-generated vs. self-sourced pipeline ratios.

  * **Attribution Infrastructure:** We configure objects to track the full funnel from hand-raiser to closed won with all of the detail you need instead of having to join a bunch of different reports together.

  * **System Optimization:** We don't leave you to go execute alone. We're partners with you along your growth trajectory.




### 5\. **ACCELERATE** | _Executing the Value Creation Plan_

The ultimate goal of a 30-day audit isn't a 100-page slide deck. It's an actionable, prioritized execution roadmap to take you through your current inflection point (see where the name comes from?) and your next round or M&A.

  * **Quick Wins vs. Strategic Overhauls:** We isolate high-impact, low-effort levers (ex: automated renewal workflows) for days 31-60.

  * **Embedding In Your Teams:** We work closely with your teams to execute against the roadmap and enable your teams to perform at a higher level. We're in your Slack and weekly team meetings.

  * **The Valuation Impact:** Accelerated EBITDA expansion and clear line-of-sight to non-linear ARR growth.
